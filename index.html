Notion embeds

Single-file widgets for Notion /embed blocks. Every option is a URL query param — a sandboxed cross-origin iframe can't read anything from the page around it.

Clock → clock.html

Analog face, Los Angeles unless you say otherwise. Click to cycle timezones with ?zones=.

Calendar → calendar.html

Month grid with today marked, Sunday-first. ?start=mon for the ISO week.

Both default to the green card. ?fill=false drops it and lets the page through; ?fill=color paints it something else — the text and highlight recolor themselves to stay readable on whatever you pick.
